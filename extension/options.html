Apply Kit

Changes save automatically.

Your profile

Fills matching fields on application forms. Leave anything blank to skip it.

Resume

Attached to resume upload fields when you fill a form. The PDF is kept on this device only, because sync storage is too small for it. Its name and date sync, so your other device can tell you when its copy is out of date.

Google Drive, Google Docs, Dropbox, or a direct link to a PDF. Anyone with the link must be able to view it. Firefox asks once for permission to reach that site; nothing is downloaded until you tap Download.

Extra fields

For questions the profile doesn’t cover. When a field’s label contains one of the keywords, this value is filled in.

Saved answers

Longer answers you reuse. Similar questions on a form get these as suggestions.

Sync and backup

This syncs between your devices when Firefox Sync is on with Add-ons selected. Changes can take a few minutes to reach the other device.
